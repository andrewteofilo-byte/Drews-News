Tune in next picks the best story in markets whose newscast is coming up (your selected windows, or any window if none are selected). Stars pin a market to the top and to the Pinned option in the scan list. The Porter log is saved on this device. Tap "Why" on a card to see how its rating was built. Must-watch is a modelled score (story type, freshness, deaths and injuries named in the headlines, how many outlets have it, national pickup, whether a local station is already on it), not an observed one. A story older than 3 hours stays on the board only if it clears the big-story test: two or more killed, five or more hurt, a mass-casualty or aircraft story, a major story carried by three or more outlets, six or more outlets on any story, or two national outlets. Spanish-language stories are translated before they are classified, with the original kept under the headline; if the translator is unreachable they are read through a small newsroom glossary instead, which is marked on the card. Stations, newsrooms, owners, live stream links, newscast times and the National Desk rules come from Drew's Secret 11.5. The play symbol on a chip opens that station's stream page. Weekday times are the verified census; weekend times and stations outside the census follow 11.5's modelled pattern and are marked with an asterisk. Stations with no local news are dimmed. Stations shown are who would carry the story, not confirmation that they have.
